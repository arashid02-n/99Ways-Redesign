# 99Ways palette - v1.0

| Role | Light value | Dark-field adaptation | Logo use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Primary neutral | Graphite #292B2A | Warm White #F4F3EF | Bodies and Ways |
| Inquiry | Blue #3558B8 | Inverse blue #A6BAFF | Pupils |
| Supporting information | Muted #676B65 | Inverse muted #B5B8B1 | Outside the logo |
| Action / favorable outcome | Green #147D40 | Inverse green #6BD98D | Outside the logo |

The standard logo uses only the neutral and inquiry roles. Performance green does not enter the logo. Background fields are not embedded in transparent logo assets. Platform tiles and profile canvases are the documented exception.
